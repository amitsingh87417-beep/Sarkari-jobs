Practice tips
  • Accuracy first: aim for consistent 95%+ accuracy before increasing speed.
  • Keep fingers on the home row and avoid looking at the keyboard.
  • Review repeated mistakes and practise weak keys for 5–10 minutes.
  • Use the same language and keyboard layout expected in your exam.

6. Important exam note

This is an independent practice simulator, not an official exam portal. Exam profiles provide practice guidance only; they do not reproduce every official test rule. SSC DEST, SSC CHSL, RRB, JKSSB, CPCT, court recruitment and stenography tests may use different passages, keyboards, timings, qualifying criteria and evaluation methods. Always check the latest official notification and practise with the required keyboard layout. Hindi Unicode typing is not the same as legacy Remington/GAIL/Inscript layouts; choose the layout required by your exam.
