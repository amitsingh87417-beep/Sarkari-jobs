This is a practice simulator. Exam profiles are practice guidance, not official settings or an official result/certificate. Always verify the current recruitment notification, required keyboard layout, duration and evaluation method. CGL DEST and stenography require separate task formats beyond paragraph typing.

Data:
Typing history is saved in the browser's localStorage on the current device/browser. It does not automatically sync between devices.
